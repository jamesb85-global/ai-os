# CIM / Listing

Authorship plus render. Verified numbers in. Narratives, listing copy, and a branded PDF out.

The **CIM** (confidential information memorandum) is the packet a serious buyer gets under NDA. **Listing** is the copy around it: the web blurb, the email, the one-liner that makes the right buyer ask for the CIM.

## What the agent does

It writes from the sheet the broker already signed. CIM body: thesis, operations, levers that are actually in the Q&A. Listing assets from the same source so the website and the PDF don't tell two stories. A renderer stamps the branded PDF — their brand file, their field map.

It doesn't invent collections, seller discretionary earnings, or asking price.

## Human in the loop

Asking price, multiples, and what goes to buyers under NDA stay with the broker. Nothing leaves until they sign off.
