# Escrow onboarding

**Absorbs:** Signed LOI → portal fields, escrow-open draft, buyer intro, seller intro. Contingency-date math. Same day.

**Stays human:** TC review before send. Counsel, lender, and vendor choices.

**In:** Executed LOI, listing agreement if present, agent deal email.

**Out:** Four drafts. No addresses in the copies. Lease section only when the buyer is not taking the building.

**Install:** Their portal, escrow partner, and TC voice. The fuller command center after kickoff is story only — this station is the start. The desk then runs the deal through close.
