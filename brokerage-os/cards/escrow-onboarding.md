# Escrow onboarding

The offer is accepted. That's the win. Then the clock starts.

Someone has to open escrow, load the portal, email the buyer, email the seller, and get the contingency dates right — from a signed letter of intent (the **LOI**: the short signed document that says price, earnest money, timing, and what has to happen before close). On most desks that's the transaction coordinator, rebuilding the deal by hand from a PDF, or the broker doing it because the coordinator is already in three other files.

The bottleneck is the gap between "we're in contract" and "escrow is actually open." Days disappear. The buyer waits. The seller's front desk starts taking lender calls they shouldn't.

## What this station is

Escrow onboarding is the same-day kickoff. Not the whole transaction through close — the start. Four drafts, from the LOI, so the deal is live this afternoon.

## What the agent does

It pulls parties, prices, earnest money, financing, and date math from the signed LOI. Practice only or building too — that changes whether the lease belongs in the intros. Commission from the listing agreement if it's in the pack.

Four artifacts:

1. **Portal fields** — what the firm's deal system needs to open the file
2. **Escrow-open** — draft to the escrow partner, LOI attached
3. **Buyer intro** — earnest money timing, data room, lease if they're not buying the building
4. **Seller intro** — escrow is opening, lenders will ask for more, route the buyer through the coordinator not the front desk

No street addresses in the copies. Counsel and vendor choices left blank for the humans who actually pick them.

## Human in the loop

The transaction coordinator reads all four before anything sends. They choose counsel, lender, and vendors. The agent drafts the kickoff. The desk still owns the send.

## What that unlocks

Contract to open the same day, not when someone finds two hours. The coordinator stops retyping the same packet on every deal. The broker isn't the email layer. Capacity here is simple: more files can be in escrow at once because kickoff isn't the thing that waits.
