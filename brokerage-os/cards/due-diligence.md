# Due Diligence

Source documents in, a structured review out. Not a valuation. Not a CIM.

## What the agent does

It reads the pack — P&L, tax returns, lease, payroll. It maps who actually produces (owner, associates, hygiene). It marks add-backs only where the document supports them and leaves the rest as questions.

It flags what a careful buyer will flag, then writes the questions for the doctor. Generate and verify are separate passes. It doesn't trust the recap until the source pack agrees.

## Human in the loop

The agent never prices. What to believe, what to ask, whether this file advances — that's the broker.
