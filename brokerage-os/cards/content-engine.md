# Content Engine

**Absorbs:** Weekly outline → blog → social/newsletter, plus webinar → clips + written follow-on, in the principal's voice.

**Stays human:** The point of view. Outline approval before a draft. Hook sign-off before clips publish.

**In:** Topic (or a recording). Voice file.

**Out:** Outline, piece, atomized posts, newsletter teaser; or transcript, clip briefs, YouTube pack.

**Install:** Voice and topic library on their stack. Same station for written and video — do not ship two products.
