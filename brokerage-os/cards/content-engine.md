# Content Engine

The desk's publishing rhythm. Written and video, same station, same voice.

## What the agent does

You give it a topic or a recording. It already has the principal's voice file — how they talk, what they won't say, the standard of the firm.

From a topic: outline first, then the full piece, then LinkedIn, email, and a newsletter teaser. One idea, three surfaces.

From a webinar: transcript, four standalone clip briefs (hook, number, story, what to do next), captions, a YouTube pack, and a written follow-on.

## Human in the loop

The principal approves the outline before a draft exists and signs off the hooks before a clip publishes. The engine doesn't post.
