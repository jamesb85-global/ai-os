# Walkthrough

Five stations, one file, in order. You don't upload a deal. You sit with the desk and watch it work.

At the end of each station you see the packet — the list, the findings, the CIM, the escrow drafts — and you decide what happens next: hold, rewrite, send to the coordinator, or advance the file.

Packets here are samples. The day they install, their documents meet the same stations.

A live tour on the site comes next. Until then, this folder is the OS on paper: the [fulfillment engine](stations.md), the [five stations](README.md), and [how it's built](how-its-built.md).
