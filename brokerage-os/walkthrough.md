# Walkthrough

You don't upload a deal. You sit with the desk and watch it work.

That's the walkthrough. Five stations, one file, in order. At the end of each station you see the packet — the list, the findings, the CIM, the escrow drafts — and you decide what happens next. Hold. Rewrite. Send to the coordinator. Advance the file.

You're not chatting with a general assistant. You're walking the fulfillment engine the way a principal would on a live install: here's what this station took in, here's what it produced, here's the gate.

Nothing in the room is their real practice. Sample packets, so the shape is visible. The day they install, their documents meet the same stations.

A live tour on the site comes next. Until then, this folder is the OS on paper: the [fulfillment engine](stations.md), the [five stations](README.md), and [how it's built](how-its-built.md).
