# How it's built

Brokerage OS is the method. The desk is theirs. Five stations, the packets they produce, and the rule that a person still signs off. Their portal, fonts, sheet schemas, and deal book stay with the firm.

## Their stack

It plugs into the CRM they already have, the documents they already keep, and the escrow partner they already trust. Adapters, not a migration. The brand file, field map, and voice are the principal's.

## A person at every gate

The OS drafts. It doesn't send. It doesn't decide who's a hot listing, set asking price, show a buyer the CIM, or email escrow until a person has looked.

If a station can't do the job without a human judgment, it stops and asks.
