# How it's built

The OS is the method. The desk is theirs.

That line is the whole design.

Brokerage OS is not a copy of someone else's brokerage. It's not their portal, their fonts, their sheet schemas, their deal book. Those stay with the firm that paid for them. What travels is the operating system: five stations, the packets they produce, and the rule that a person still signs off.

## Their stack, not a replacement

It doesn't ask the desk to move into a new tool. It plugs into the CRM they already have, the documents they already keep, the escrow partner they already trust. Adapters, not a migration.

The brand file is theirs. The field map is theirs. The voice is the principal's.

## A person at every gate

The OS drafts. It doesn't send.

It doesn't decide who's a hot listing. It doesn't set asking price. It doesn't tell a buyer the story until the broker has read the CIM. It doesn't email escrow until the transaction coordinator has looked at the drafts.

If a station can't do the job without a human judgment, it stops and asks.

## Sample is sample

Numbers in this folder are invented. They're here so you can see the shape of a packet — findings, a ranked list, listing copy, an escrow kickoff — without looking at a real practice.

A live walkthrough doesn't take their files. The install is where their documents meet the system.

## What that gives the desk

A principal who can leave the room and the work still moves. A coordinator who isn't retyping the same four emails. A listing that sounds like the firm because the voice file and the verified numbers are the same source.

The instance is theirs. The OS is what you install.
