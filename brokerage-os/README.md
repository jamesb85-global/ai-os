# Brokerage OS

A practice-brokerage desk usually runs through one person. Every listing, every P&L, every LOI waits on the same head.

Brokerage OS is the system that takes that load.

Five AI stations sit on the desk. They find the next seller, keep the firm's voice in market, read the books, write the CIM, and open escrow the day the offer is accepted. The broker still makes the call. The OS does the work that used to sit in the queue.

It installs onto the firm's CRM, documents, and escrow process. Their stack. Their brand. Their judgment.

## What it does

**[Content Engine](cards/content-engine.md)**  
The desk sounds like the principal even when the principal isn't writing. One topic — or one recorded session — becomes the blog, the posts, the newsletter, and the clips. Outline first. Hooks signed off. Then it ships in their voice.

**[Seller Origination](cards/seller-origination.md)**  
The next listing doesn't have to come from a referral. Public signals (license tenure, review velocity, succession) become a ranked call list, refreshed on a cadence. The broker still picks who to call. The list is already waiting.

**[Due Diligence](cards/due-diligence.md)**  
Drop in the P&L, the tax returns, the lease, the payroll. Get back a provider map, add-backs that the documents actually support, red flags, and the questions to ask the doctor. It reads. It doesn't price. The file either advances or it doesn't — that's the broker.

**[CIM / Listing](cards/cim-listing.md)**  
Verified numbers in. Confidential information memorandum, listing copy, and a branded PDF out. Thesis, growth levers, marketing assets — written once, rendered from the same source. Asking price and what buyers see under NDA stay with the broker.

**[Escrow onboarding](cards/escrow-onboarding.md)**  
Offer accepted. Same day: portal fields, escrow-open draft, buyer intro, seller intro. Contingency dates already math'd from the LOI. The transaction coordinator reviews. Then the deal is live.

## How it operates

Each station is a specialist agent. It takes in what the desk already has and returns a packet someone can act on. A person signs off before anything leaves the building — a post, a call, a valuation, a CIM, an escrow email.

That's the product: a brokerage desk that runs like an operating system, with the principal still at the wheel.
