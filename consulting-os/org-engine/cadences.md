# Cadence

The org engine runs on a week. Same stations, same gates. The principal decides whether work cleared the bar, not when it happens.

**Weekday morning — Hunt.** Fit and pain shape. Cards land at the hub. No person names.

**Friday morning — Keep / skip.** The principal marks the hunt recap. Keep becomes eligible for list-prep. Skip dies.

**Sunday — Marketing packet.** Two drafts for the week. The principal pastes. The engine doesn't publish.

**Tuesday / Thursday — Paste reminder.** A nudge. Not a new post.

**Friday / Saturday / Sunday — Hub gates.** Second eyes, list quality, content quality. Quiet if it passed.

**Weekday evening — Handoff close.** The coordinator writes the state of the week.

**Sunday afternoon — Spine.** The coordinator is the sole writer of the org's source of truth.
