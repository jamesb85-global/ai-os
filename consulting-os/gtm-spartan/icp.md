# ICP

Scoring policy for hunt. Each card gets a fit (0–5), a why, and a next action: keep, skip, or ask the principal.

The ICP is founder-led firms where an OS install changes the week: the founder is still the routing layer, the team is small enough that a packet lands, and they want drafts and lists — not a robot that posts and sends. A vertical-only identity as the public face of the firm is a skip.

Hunt doesn't write a person name into the score. A 5 is worth a human gate, not a send. Apply doesn't run until keep exists.
