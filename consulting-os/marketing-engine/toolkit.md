# Toolkit

A content pass is a compile step, not a feed.

The agent doesn't "make posts." It produces a **packet**: two drafts, each with a job.

**Hook** — one line that earns the rest.  
**Body** — something a founder-operator can use this week.  
**Close** — optional. A next step, not a pitch deck.  
**Source** — why this exists: a week in the OS, a pattern, a question. Grounded, so the generator doesn't freewheel a thought-leadership persona.

If the usefulness bar fails, the pass isn't done. Rewrite until a reader would change their week, or kill the draft before it reaches the coordinator.

That's the toolkit: structured generation, a quality gate, a packet the hub can actually orchestrate. The principal still decides what the market hears.
