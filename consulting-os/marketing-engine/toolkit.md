# Toolkit

A content pass produces a **packet**: two drafts, each with a job.

**Hook** — one line that earns the rest.  
**Body** — something a founder-operator can use this week.  
**Close** — optional. A next step, not a pitch.  
**Source** — why this exists: a week in the OS, a pattern, a question.

If the usefulness bar fails, rewrite or kill the draft before it reaches the coordinator. The principal still decides what the market hears.
